Program Overview

Objective: Prove measurable ROI from the Omniscient Real Estate Growth OS within 90 days, using 2 offices and 6–10 agents as the controlled test environment — before making a decision on full brokerage rollout.

Philosophy: One closed transaction pays for the entire pilot. Everything above that is upside.

Pilot scope:

  • 2 offices selected (recommended: Spring Lake + Manasquan)
  • 6–10 agents (mix of experienced + newer agents preferred)
  • Full AI voice, CRM automation, and dashboard deployed
  • Weekly reporting to leadership
  • Clear go/no-go criteria at Day 90

Pilot Offices — Selection Criteria

Recommended offices for pilot:

Office Reason
Spring Lake Highest average transaction value, flagship brand presence, strong agent base
Manasquan High lead volume from web + Zillow, good mix of buyer/seller activity, manageable team size

Alternative: Substitute either with Point Pleasant Beach (highest web traffic) or Bay Head (highest price point) based on leadership preference.

Agent Selection — 6–10 Agents

Recommended composition:

  • 2–3 Top Producers (Platinum/Gold tier) — establishes benchmark data
  • 3–4 Mid-tier agents — largest opportunity for lift
  • 1–2 Newer agents (< 2 years) — shows onboarding value of system

Selection criteria:

  • Willing to adopt new tools (not resistant to tech)
  • Available for 2-hour onboarding session
  • Agreed to use GHL as primary CRM for pilot period
  • Willing to receive AI-generated leads and respond per protocol

What agents need to know:

  • The system will respond to their leads for them in the first 8 seconds
  • They will receive notifications and can take over any conversation at any time
  • Their performance data will be visible to management (with advance notice)
  • The goal is to help them close more — not to monitor or penalize

Week 2 — Agent Onboarding

Session format: 2-hour group onboarding per office (in-person or Zoom)

Onboarding agenda:

Time Topic
0:00 – 0:20 Welcome & Why This System Exists — the $4.2M opportunity
0:20 – 0:45 Live demo: Watch AI respond to a fake lead in real time
0:45 – 1:15 GHL mobile app walkthrough — lead cards, pipeline, calendar

Agents leave with:

  • Web forms routing to GHL

  • Leadership dashboard live and accessible
  • Daily check-in call between Omniscient team and office managers (15 min)

Phase 2 — Optimize & Measure (Weeks 4–8)

Weekly Cadence

  • 30-minute check-in call with office managers

Ongoing:

  • AI scripts tuned based on call transcripts (typically Week 4–5)

  • Lead routing logic optimized based on agent response patterns

Phase 3 — Results & Decision (Weeks 9–12)

Day 90 Presentation to Leadership

Omniscient presents a full 90-day ROI report including:

  1. Lead volume captured — total leads that entered the system across both pilot offices
  2. Conversion funnel analysis — lead → contact → qualify → appt → showing → contract → close rates
  3. Response time transformation — before (agent manual) vs. after (AI-powered) average response
  4. Appointments generated — total appointments booked by AI vs. booked by agents manually
  5. Revenue attributed — closed transactions where lead entered via Omniscient OS
  6. Projected revenue missed without system — based on leads that were captured after hours, on weekends, or from previously abandoned sources
  7. Agent adoption scores — who engaged with the system and how it affected their numbers
  8. Full rollout recommendation — timeline, investment, and projected 12-month ROI for all 14 offices

Go / No-Go Criteria

The pilot is considered a success and full rollout is recommended if any 3 of the following 5 conditions are met:

Criteria Target
Appointment booking rate ≥ 25% of qualified leads
Avg lead response time ≤ 2 minutes (AI + agent combined)
Closed transactions attributed ≥ 2 per pilot office over 90 days
Agent adoption rate ≥ 70% of pilot agents actively using GHL
After-hours lead recovery ≥ 50% of after-hours inquiries resulting in next-day contact

In practice, we expect all 5 criteria to be met by Week 8.

Agent Onboarding Steps — Individual

Each pilot agent follows this onboarding sequence:

Step 1 — Before onboarding session:

  • Receive welcome email from Omniscient with GHL login link
  • Download GHL mobile app (iOS/Android)
  • Connect Google/Outlook calendar (instructions included)

Step 2 — Onboarding session (2 hours):

  • Watch live AI demo
  • Learn lead card navigation
  • Set up notification preferences
  • Practice: receive test lead, review AI transcript, add note, move pipeline stage

Step 3 — First week in-system:

  • Receive first real leads via push notification
  • Daily 15-minute check-in available with Omniscient support (optional)
  • Any questions escalated to dedicated agent support Slack channel or text line

Step 4 — Ongoing:

  • Weekly performance digest emailed every Monday
  • Monthly coaching note from Omniscient on top improvement opportunity

Daily Workflow — Agent Experience

Morning (8:00–9:00 AM):

  • Review overnight leads in GHL inbox
  • Call any hot leads that came in after hours (AI already responded — agent adds human touch)
  • Check appointments for the day
  • Review any nurture sequence replies that need human response

Midday:

  • Respond to any new lead notifications (AI handles first contact — agent notified on hot leads)
  • Update pipeline stages for active leads
  • Log showing notes after appointments

End of day:

  • Review pipeline for stalled leads
  • Check any upcoming appointments for tomorrow
  • Confirm any appointments needing reschedule

Weekly:

  • Review Monday performance report
  • Reach out to one "cool" nurture lead manually (system flags best candidates)
  • Note any feedback for Omniscient team (script improvements, friction points)
