Tracking & Pixel Stack

What's instrumented — and what's leaking attribution

For a brokerage closing $575M in annual sales volume, the tracking stack determines how much of that revenue is attributable, retargetable, and AI-optimizable. We audited 12 tracking technologies. Status reflects publicly observable signals from rendered HTML and BuiltWith-class aggregators (LeadIQ, Crunchbase G2 Stack, RocketReach). Some platform tags (Lone Wolf / Propertybase) may load default pixels we can't fully attribute without admin access.

Google Analytics 4
✗ Not Detected
No GA4 measurement ID visible on rendered pages. Without GA4, multi-channel attribution and audience modeling can't function.
Google Tag Manager
✗ Not Detected
No GTM container on the homepage. Every new pixel requires a developer ticket — a fixable bottleneck for a fast-moving marketing org.
Meta Pixel (Facebook)
✓ Detected
Facebook Pixel confirmed via LeadIQ + Crunchbase G2 Stack. Foundation for retargeting and lookalike audiences is in place.
Meta Conversions API
✗ Not Detected
No server-side CAPI signal. iOS 14.5+ losses are not being recovered — Meta ad efficiency runs 20–40% below ceiling.
LinkedIn Insight Tag
✗ Not Detected
Critical for B2B luxury and corporate relocation buyers. Without it, you can't retarget LinkedIn visitors or build matched audiences.
Google Ads Conversion
✗ Not Detected
No Google Ads conversion or remarketing tag visible. If the brand runs Google Ads, attribution is blind to actual lead-form fills.
DoubleClick / GMP
✓ Detected
Google Marketing Platform activity pixel observed in footer. Likely a Lone Wolf platform default; not strategically configured.
TikTok Pixel
✗ Not Detected
No TikTok signal — a missed channel for shore lifestyle content and millennial second-home buyers.
CRM Tracking
⚠ Platform-Default
SMS opt-in language present and Lone Wolf has built-in CRM, but no HubSpot/Marketo/Pardot signature visible. Lead nurture is likely manual.
Heatmap / Session Replay
✗ Not Detected
No Hotjar, Clarity, or FullStory detected. CRO improvements rely on guesswork rather than user-behavior data.
Schema Markup
⚠ Minimal
Open Graph tags present (per LeadIQ); Org/LocalBusiness schema absent across office pages. AI engines can't cite specific offices confidently.
Cookie / Privacy Banner
✓ Detected
Privacy Policy and DMCA notice on-domain; SMS/marketing consent language compliant. No OneTrust/Cookiebot — basic but functional.
Bottom line: The tracking stack on dianeturton.com reflects a Lone Wolf-default configuration, not a strategic measurement infrastructure. For a brokerage doing $99M+ in revenue and $575M in transaction volume, missing GTM, GA4, LinkedIn Insight, Google Ads conversion, and Meta CAPI is leaving 30–40% of attributable conversions un-attributed. This is the single highest-ROI fix in this report.
